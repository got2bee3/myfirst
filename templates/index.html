Data and calculation notes

Source: nflverse schedule data. Sportsbook and opening/closing designation are unknown. Prices are not verified DraftKings prices. No missing spread price is replaced with −110. A cover means the team’s final score plus its spread exceeds the opponent’s score; equality is a push, even if the game itself was not tied. Moneyline ties and spread pushes return the stake and are excluded from settled ROI stake and expected-outcome comparisons. Spread expectations normalize both spread prices to remove vig and refer to non-push outcomes, not a prediction model. Historical franchise aliases are combined. Home/Away follows source designation and may describe a neutral-site game.
