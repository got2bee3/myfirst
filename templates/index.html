Data and calculation notes

Source: nflverse schedule data. Original sportsbook and opening/closing designation are unknown in this snapshot. These prices are not verified DraftKings prices. Market expectations normalize both implied probabilities to remove vig. Ties are treated as void bets with zero profit and excluded from settled stake and expected-win comparisons. Historical franchise aliases are combined. “Home/Away” follows source designation and may describe a neutral-site game.

Snapshot retrieved: {{ meta.get('retrieved_at','Not recorded') }} · Source dataset. No model predictions or live sportsbook odds are used.
